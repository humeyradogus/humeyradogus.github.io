ÇENGEL BULMACA

· PRIVACY POLICY ·

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Çengel Bulmaca mobile app (the “App”) handles and how. Questions can be sent to humeyradogus@outlook.com.

Information we collect

The App has no accounts, no servers and no analytics tools. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, location or contacts.

Your game progress (solved levels, stars, times, hint balance, language and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show optional rewarded video ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect the following from your device:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (the IDFA on iOS — only with your permission),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app usage data,
  • Diagnostic and performance data.

Google processes this data under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for personalized ads. If you decline, ads may still be shown but are not personalized. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

In these regions, consent for advertising is collected with Google's consent management tool (UMP). You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad content is limited to a PG (parental guidance) rating.

Data deletion

Because we do not store any data about you, there is no account to delete. You can remove the game data on your device by deleting the App. For advertising data collected by Google, use the controls Google provides.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
